## Episode 34: Sun Ce's Legacy

Zhuge Liang's words strike Liu Bei like a revelation. Unable to resist Liu Bei's sincerity any longer, Zhuge Liang agrees to leave his cottage and serve as advisor. The scene shifts east to Wu, where Sun Ce, the young conqueror who built the Sun family's domain south of the Yangtze, is ambushed by assassins during a hunting expedition and mortally wounded.

Sun Quan rushes back to the general's residence. Following Zhang Zhao's instructions, he seals the roads and suppresses the news. On his deathbed, Sun Ce entrusts the governance of the three river provinces to his younger brother. Sun Quan, only eighteen years old, inherits a territory surrounded by enemies on three sides and staffed entirely by his father's and brother's veterans. Zhou Yu, stationed at the front line in Baqiu, races back to mourn. In the funeral hall, he declines Sun Quan's offer of supreme command but pledges lifelong loyalty to the young lord.

Meanwhile, in Xinye, Liu Bei treats Zhuge Liang with the reverence due a teacher, spending his days in deep discussion. Guan Yu and Zhang Fei, jealous of this newcomer's influence, simmer with resentment. Then word arrives: Cao Cao has dispatched Xiahou Dun with an army aimed straight at Xinye.

**Historical significance:** Sun Ce's assassination in 200 AD is well documented in historical records, though the show compresses the timeline by placing it alongside Zhuge Liang's recruitment (historically 207 AD). Sun Ce was genuinely ambushed by retainers of Xu Gong, a local official he had killed. His deathbed charge to Sun Quan and Zhou Yu's loyalty pledge are grounded in the historical sources, though the specific dialogue is dramatized. The show's placement of these events together serves a narrative purpose: it introduces Sun Quan as a ruler in the same breath that Liu Bei gains his strategist, setting up the eventual alliance between the two.

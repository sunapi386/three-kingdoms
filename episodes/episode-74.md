## Episode 74: The Seven Steps Poem

In the ancestral temple, the Han Emperor Xian weeps before the tablets of his forebears, terrified that the Han dynasty will end on his watch. His fears are well-founded.

Cao Zhang, commanding 100,000 troops, marches toward the capital ostensibly to mourn his father but perhaps to seize the throne. Cao Pi is alarmed. Sima Yi volunteers to handle the crisis and rides out to confront Cao Zhang directly. In a tense confrontation in Cao Zhang's military tent, Sima Yi browbeats the prince into surrendering his command and entering the capital alone to pay respects. It is Sima Yi's first major political triumph and a preview of the patience and ruthlessness that will eventually devour the Wei dynasty itself.

With that threat neutralized, Sima Yi advises Cao Pi to test Cao Zhi's loyalty by summoning him to mourn. Cao Pi rewards Sima Yi with the gift of a serving woman named Jingshu. When Cao Zhi arrives, Cao Pi accuses him of disrespect toward their late father and threatens execution. Their mother intervenes furiously, and Cao Pi promises no harm. But the next day in open court, he issues his challenge: compose a poem within seven steps, or die. Cao Zhi, tears streaming, recites the immortal Seven Steps Poem: "Boiling beans over a beanstalk fire / The beans weep within the pot / Born from the same root as we are / Why do you burn me with such haste?" Even Cao Pi is moved. Cao Zhi is spared but permanently sidelined.

**Historical significance:** The Seven Steps Poem (七步诗) is one of the most famous poems in the Chinese language, though its authenticity is debated. It first appears in the *Shishuo Xinyu* (5th century anecdote collection), not in the formal histories. The dramatic framing with the seven-step deadline comes from the 14th-century novel. Historically, Cao Zhi was indeed politically marginalized after Cao Pi's succession, moved between fiefdoms and kept under surveillance until his death in 232 CE. Sima Yi's role in resolving the Cao Zhang crisis is dramatized here but reflects his historical emergence as the indispensable advisor to the Cao family. The series wisely gives Sima Yi prominent screen time in these episodes, planting seeds for the endgame decades later.

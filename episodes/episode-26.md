## Episode 26: Reunion at Ancient City

Guan Yu arrives at the ancient city (古城) where Zhang Fei has established himself. Zhang Fei charges out to confront his brother, furious at what he sees as a betrayal for serving Cao Cao. After a tense standoff and some dramatic complications, the two brothers reconcile and put their differences behind them.

Liu Bei, who has slipped away from Yuan Shao's camp, encounters Zhao Yun on the road. The three sworn brothers and Zhao Yun converge on the ancient city for an emotional reunion. Their small but fiercely loyal band is whole again.

The narrative then shifts north, where Yuan Shao's three sons arrive with reinforcements for the coming war against Cao Cao. A fierce dispute breaks out among the brothers over who should command the army, but Yuan Shao settles the matter by taking personal command. The episode ends with Yuan Shao leading a massive host of 700,000 soldiers south toward Guandu for the decisive confrontation with Cao Cao.

**Historical significance:** The "Reunion at Gucheng" (古城聚义) is a beloved scene from the novel but has no basis in the historical record. Liu Bei did eventually reunite with Guan Yu and Zhang Fei, but the dramatic circumstances are fictional. The quarrel among Yuan Shao's three sons (Yuan Tan, Yuan Xi, Yuan Shang), however, foreshadows a very real historical problem: after Yuan Shao's death, his sons' infighting would destroy the Yuan clan from within. Yuan Shao's army at Guandu was historically large (estimates range from 100,000 to several hundred thousand), though the 700,000 figure is a novelistic exaggeration.

## Episode 52: The Marriage Proposal

Sun Quan sends an envoy to propose marriage, inviting Liu Bei to travel south to Jianye for the wedding, citing Lady Wu's (Wu Guotai's) advanced age as the reason Liu Bei must come in person. Liu Bei hesitates and faces strong opposition from his advisors and sworn brothers, but ultimately decides to accept. He genuinely hopes the marriage alliance will strengthen the Sun-Liu pact against Cao Cao. Before departing, Liu Bei transfers full military and political authority over Jingzhou to Zhuge Liang, which alarms Guan Yu and Zhang Fei, who fear Zhuge Liang might overstep his bounds.

Liu Bei addresses their concerns by granting Zhuge Liang a sealed order authorizing punishment of anyone who disobeys. Following Zhuge Liang's instructions, Liu Bei makes a lavish public display of betrothal gifts upon arriving in Wu territory, ensuring that the news of the marriage spreads far and wide. When Lady Wu learns of the arrangement through public gossip rather than from her own son, she is furious. The resulting confrontation between Lady Wu and Sun Quan creates a rift between Sun Quan and Zhou Yu, whose secret scheme has been exposed by the publicity.

**Historical significance:** The marriage between Liu Bei and Sun Shangxiang did occur historically around 209 CE. It was a political marriage meant to cement the alliance after Red Cliffs. The novel's version, where Zhuge Liang gives sealed stratagems (the famous "three brocade bags" or 锦囊妙计) to help Liu Bei navigate the trap, is fictional embellishment. The character of Wu Guotai (Lady Wu) is a composite figure in the novel. Historically, Sun Quan's mother Lady Wu had already died before these events. The episode dramatizes the fundamental tension in the Sun-Liu relationship: allies against Cao Cao, but competitors for the same territory.

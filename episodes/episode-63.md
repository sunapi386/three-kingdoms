## Episode 63: Zhang Song Offers a Map

The focus shifts to the southwest. Zhang Song, a counselor serving the weak lord Liu Zhang in Yi Province (Sichuan), travels to Cao Cao's court hoping to form an alliance. Cao Cao, arrogant and dismissive, humiliates the physically unimpressive Zhang Song. Furious at this treatment, Zhang Song departs empty-handed. On his return journey he encounters Liu Bei, who treats him with warmth and genuine respect. Moved by this contrast, Zhang Song secretly presents Liu Bei with a detailed military map of Yi Province and urges him to take the territory from Liu Zhang.

Back in Yi Province, Zhang Song and his ally Fa Zheng begin laying the groundwork for Liu Bei's entry into the region. They convince Liu Zhang that an alliance with Liu Bei is the best defense against Zhang Lu's forces threatening from Hanzhong to the north. Liu Zhang, persuaded by their arguments, sends an invitation for Liu Bei to bring his army into Yi Province. Liu Bei hesitates, torn between ambition and his commitment to righteousness, since Liu Zhang is a fellow member of the Liu imperial clan.

**Historical significance:** Zhang Song's mission to Cao Cao and subsequent defection to Liu Bei is based on historical events around 211-212 CE. The map of Yi Province is a famous detail from the novel. Historically, Fa Zheng and Zhang Song did conspire to invite Liu Bei into Yi Province, and Liu Zhang did welcome Liu Bei as an ally against Zhang Lu. This episode marks the beginning of Liu Bei's campaign to take the Sichuan basin, which would become the territorial foundation of his Shu Han kingdom.

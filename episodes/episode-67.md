## Episode 67: Ma Chao Pledges Allegiance

The duel between Zhang Fei and Ma Chao rages on without a clear victor. Liu Bei, watching from a distance, recognizes Ma Chao as an extraordinary warrior and wants him on his side. Zhuge Liang, having arrived in Yi Province, devises a scheme to turn Ma Chao away from Zhang Lu. He sends agents to bribe Zhang Lu's advisor Yang Song, who whispers slander about Ma Chao's loyalty into Zhang Lu's ear. Zhang Lu takes the bait and orders Ma Chao to withdraw from the front lines under the pretext of a Cao Cao invasion. When Ma Chao refuses the recall order, Zhang Lu is furious and sets impossible conditions for his return. Ma Chao, trapped between an ungrateful master and a hostile enemy, is left stranded outside the walls of Hanzhong.

Zhuge Liang personally visits Ma Chao's camp and persuades him to join Liu Bei. Ma Chao swears allegiance. His defection changes the balance of power immediately: when Ma Chao leads his fearsome cavalry against Chengdu, Liu Zhang is terrified. Resistance crumbles. Liu Zhang opens the gates and surrenders the city. In Yi Province's government hall, Liu Bei sincerely invites the loyalist minister Huang Quan to serve the cause of restoring the Han dynasty. Huang Quan, moved to tears, accepts. Yi Province now belongs to Liu Bei, who governs it with a firm hand. Zhuge Liang observes privately that Liu Bei has transformed: the wandering exile has become a sovereign.

**Historical significance:** Ma Chao's defection to Liu Bei in 214 CE was a real and significant event. Historically, Ma Chao had been mistreated by Zhang Lu and approached Liu Bei on his own initiative; the novel and show add Zhuge Liang's machinations for dramatic effect. Liu Zhang's surrender of Chengdu is historical and marks the founding of Liu Bei's territorial base. The show's portrayal of Liu Bei's transformation from righteous wanderer to calculating ruler reflects the historical shift as Liu Bei assumed the mantle of a regional power. Huang Quan was indeed a notable Yi Province loyalist who eventually served both Shu Han and, after his forced defection, Wei.

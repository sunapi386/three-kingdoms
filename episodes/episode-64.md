## Episode 64: The Banquet at Fucheng

Liu Bei leads his forces into Yi Province at Liu Zhang's invitation. The two Liu clan lords meet at Fucheng for a great welcoming banquet. Liu Bei's advisor Fa Zheng privately urges him to seize Liu Zhang at the feast, but Liu Bei refuses, unwilling to break faith with a kinsman. Pang Tong, Liu Bei's chief strategist, is less restrained. He secretly arranges for the general Wei Yan to perform a sword dance during the banquet as cover for an assassination attempt on Liu Zhang. Wei Yan draws his blade in the middle of the feast, sparking a chaotic melee between soldiers of both sides. Liu Bei intervenes and stops the fighting, declaring to Zhang Ren (Liu Zhang's loyal general) that he will never commit such a dishonorable act.

When Zhang Lu's forces attack Jiameng Pass to the north, Zhang Ren suggests that Liu Zhang order Liu Bei to march out and repel the invaders as a test of his sincerity. Liu Bei accepts the assignment and successfully drives back Zhang Lu's troops, which temporarily reassures Liu Zhang. Meanwhile in the east, Sun Quan's generals urge him to seize Jing Province while Liu Bei is occupied in the west. Sun Quan refuses, but the ailing Lu Su agrees to Sun Quan's plan to promote Lu Meng as deputy commander, signaling that Wu is quietly preparing for the eventual contest over Jing Province.

**Historical significance:** The banquet at Fucheng is based on historical and novelistic accounts of Liu Bei's entry into Yi Province (212 CE). The sword dance assassination attempt is a dramatic invention echoing the famous Hongmen Banquet of early Chinese history. Historically, Liu Bei did spend roughly a year cooperating with Liu Zhang before hostilities broke out. The show uses this episode to highlight Liu Bei's central moral dilemma: his ambition for territory versus his self-image as a righteous leader. Lu Meng's quiet rise in Wu foreshadows his later, devastating capture of Jing Province from Guan Yu.

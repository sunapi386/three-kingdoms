## Episode 8: The Beauty Stratagem Begins (美人连环计)

Wang Yun, his despair now giving way to calculation, receives Lu Bu at his residence for a lavish banquet. He showers the warrior with praise for his courage and loyalty, and Lu Bu, always susceptible to flattery, drinks it in. When Diao Chan appears to serve wine and play the pipa, Lu Bu is transfixed by her beauty. In a private moment, Lu Bu confesses his deep feelings for Diao Chan to Wang Yun and, in a gesture of filial devotion, kneels and asks to call Wang Yun his adoptive father. Wang Yun, overjoyed, formally promises Diao Chan to Lu Bu in marriage.

Days later, Wang Yun hosts a separate banquet for Dong Zhuo himself. Diao Chan, elaborately dressed, performs a dance that leaves the tyrant utterly mesmerized. Dong Zhuo, even more captivated than Lu Bu had been, declares that such a beauty should be presented to the Emperor as a consort. Wang Yun agrees with exaggerated deference. But that night, the imperial sedan does not go to the palace; instead, it travels directly to Dong Zhuo's private estate at Meiwu.

When Lu Bu discovers that the woman promised to him has been taken by his adoptive father, he confronts Wang Yun in a fury. Wang Yun, playing the innocent, explains that Dong Zhuo commandeered Diao Chan and that he was powerless to resist. He subtly stokes Lu Bu's rage while maintaining his own appearance of helplessness. Lu Bu, desperate, goes to Dong Zhuo the next morning and begs his lord to give Diao Chan to him instead, but Dong Zhuo flatly refuses. Lu Bu leaves humiliated and seething.

**Historical significance:** The "chain of beauty" stratagem (连环计) is one of the most famous schemes in the novel, though it has no historical basis. Diao Chan herself may be entirely fictional; no woman by that name appears in any historical record. The *Book of the Later Han* mentions only that Lu Bu had a secret affair with one of Dong Zhuo's serving women, and that fear of discovery contributed to his willingness to turn against Dong Zhuo. The novel transformed this brief mention into an elaborate scheme engineered by Wang Yun. In the 2010 series, the adaptation adds a genuine romantic dimension between Lu Bu and Diao Chan that was absent from the novel, where Diao Chan was more purely a tool of political manipulation.

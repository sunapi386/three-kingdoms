## Episode 24: Guan Yu Slays Yan Liang and Wen Chou

Upon receiving Red Hare, Guan Yu reveals where his heart truly lies by declaring that the horse can travel a thousand li in a day, meaning he can find his brother sooner. Cao Cao's generals urge him to execute Guan Yu, but Cao Cao refuses, still hoping to win the warrior's permanent allegiance. Zhang Liao visits Guan Yu once more to convey Cao Cao's generosity, but Guan Yu simply reaffirms his commitment to Liu Bei.

At Yuan Shao's camp, Liu Bei pushes for an attack on Cao Cao, and Yuan Shao agrees. Yuan Shao sends his champion general Yan Liang to lead the vanguard. When Yan Liang cuts down two of Cao Cao's officers in succession, Guan Yu volunteers to face him. In a breathtaking single combat at the Battle of Baima (White Horse), Guan Yu charges through the enemy lines and kills Yan Liang. Yuan Shao is furious and blames Liu Bei, threatening to execute him. When Guan Yu subsequently kills the general Wen Chou as well, Liu Bei spots the banner reading "Han Shou Marquis" and realizes his brother is fighting for the other side. Yuan Shao orders Liu Bei's execution, but Liu Bei persuades Yuan Shao to let him write to Guan Yu and bring him over to their side.

**Historical significance:** Guan Yu's slaying of Yan Liang at the Battle of Baima (白马之战, 200 AD) is one of the few combat feats directly confirmed by the historical record (Sanguozhi). It was a decisive moment that broke Yuan Shao's vanguard. The killing of Wen Chou, however, is historically attributed to Cao Cao's broader tactical maneuver rather than to Guan Yu personally. The novel and series give Guan Yu credit for both kills to heighten his legend. Guan Yu was indeed awarded the title "Marquis of Han Shou" (汉寿亭侯) for his service, a title he cherished because it came from the Han Emperor rather than from Cao Cao.

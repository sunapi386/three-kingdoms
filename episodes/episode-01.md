## Episode 1: Cao Cao Presents the Sword (曹操献刀)

In the final years of the Eastern Han dynasty, the warlord Dong Zhuo has seized control of the imperial capital. He dominates the court through brute force, with his adopted son Lu Bu serving as his personal champion. The aging Grand Preceptor Wang Yun and the other senior officials are in despair, powerless against Dong Zhuo's tyranny.

Cao Cao, serving as a minor military officer (校尉), has been outwardly cooperative with Dong Zhuo while secretly harboring plans for his removal. At a birthday banquet hosted by Wang Yun, Cao Cao arrives uninvited and makes bold declarations about eliminating the tyrant. Wang Yun, outraged by the perceived recklessness, expels him from the gathering. But later that night, Wang Yun privately summons Cao Cao to an inner chamber, where Cao Cao lays out his assassination plan. Moved to tears, Wang Yun presents Cao Cao with his family's ancestral seven-star blade to aid in the deed.

The next day, Cao Cao gains an audience with Dong Zhuo under the pretext of a private meeting. Just as he draws the blade, Dong Zhuo catches sight of him in a mirror and turns. Thinking fast, Cao Cao drops to one knee and claims he has come to present the precious sword as a gift. Dong Zhuo examines the blade with suspicion, but before he can press further, Cao Cao excuses himself and flees the capital at a gallop, smashing through checkpoints along the way. When Dong Zhuo realizes the assassination attempt was real, he sends Lu Bu in hot pursuit.

**Historical significance:** The "presenting the sword" incident is drawn from Chapter 4 of the novel *Romance of the Three Kingdoms* and has no firm basis in historical records. In actual history, Cao Cao simply fled the capital after Dong Zhuo consolidated power, then raised troops in his home region. The novel and this adaptation use the scene to establish Cao Cao as bold and cunning from the very start. The series' decision to open here rather than with the Yellow Turban Rebellion or the Peach Garden Oath signals its focus on political intrigue and Cao Cao as protagonist.

## Episode 22: Liu Bei's Defeat and Despair

Ji Ping, the physician-conspirator, refuses to betray his co-conspirators even under torture and ultimately kills himself by smashing his head against a pillar. However, a servant from the Dong Cheng household betrays his master. Dong Cheng curses Cao Cao defiantly, and Cao Cao orders the extermination of his entire clan. Cao Cao then enters the palace and has Consort Dong (Dong Cheng's pregnant kinswoman) killed, further tightening his grip on the emperor.

With the conspiracy crushed, Cao Cao commits his full army to an assault on Liu Bei's position at Xuzhou, intending to destroy Liu Bei before turning to face Yuan Shao. Liu Bei sends urgent letters to Yuan Shao begging him to attack the now-emptied capital of Xuchang, but Yuan Shao, distracted by his youngest son's illness, refuses to move. Liu Bei gambles on a desperate night raid against Cao Cao's camp, but Cao Cao has anticipated the attack. Liu Bei walks into an ambush and barely escapes with his life while Cao Cao seizes Xuzhou. Alone and shattered, Liu Bei reaches a deep ravine and contemplates suicide, but is pulled back from the brink.

**Historical significance:** The destruction of the Dong Cheng conspiracy and Cao Cao's subsequent attack on Liu Bei at Xuzhou are historical events from 200 AD. The novel and series amplify Liu Bei's despair for dramatic purposes. Historically, Liu Bei's defeat at Xuzhou scattered his forces: Guan Yu was captured, and Liu Bei fled north to Yuan Shao. The killing of Consort Dong (董贵人), Dong Cheng's pregnant kinswoman, is recorded in the histories and represents one of Cao Cao's most brazen acts of intimidation against the imperial household. Some episode guides mistakenly identify the victim as Empress Fu (伏皇后), but her death at Cao Cao's hands occurs much later in the series (around episode 61, corresponding to events of 214 AD). Note: the series telescopes events that historically unfolded over several months into a single dramatic sequence.

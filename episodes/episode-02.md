## Episode 2: Chen Gong Releases Cao Cao (陈宫义释曹操)

Back in the capital, Wang Yun and the other ministers initially celebrate, believing Cao Cao has succeeded. When word arrives that Dong Zhuo is alive and furious, the tyrant issues a nationwide warrant for Cao Cao's arrest: anyone who delivers his head will be rewarded with the title of Marquis of Ten Thousand Households. Wang Yun, crushed by the failure, retreats home and attempts to take his own life. His adopted daughter Diao Chan rushes to stop him, comforting the despairing old man.

Lu Bu leads troops to search Wang Yun's residence for any trace of Cao Cao. During the search, he catches sight of Diao Chan for the first time, and the two exchange lingering glances, planting the seed for later events.

Meanwhile, in a separate narrative thread, Liu Bei, Guan Yu, and Zhang Fei swear their famous oath of brotherhood in a peach garden, pledging to live and die together. This scene, compressed into a brief sequence in this adaptation, sets up their bond without dwelling on their humble origins.

Cao Cao, fleeing east, is captured in Zhongmou county by the local magistrate Chen Gong. Chen Gong initially intends to turn Cao Cao in for the bounty, but during a late-night conversation in the jail cell, he becomes deeply impressed by Cao Cao's vision and courage. The more they talk, the more Chen Gong admires this fugitive's ambition to save the dynasty. By dawn, Chen Gong has abandoned his post, his wife, and his comfortable life to join Cao Cao on the road.

**Historical significance:** Chen Gong's release of Cao Cao is a famous episode from the novel (Chapter 4), though historical sources are sparse on their early relationship. Historically, Chen Gong was a real figure who later became Lu Bu's chief strategist. The Peach Garden Oath (桃园结义) is entirely fictional but has become one of the most iconic scenes in Chinese culture, symbolizing loyalty and brotherhood. This adaptation treats it almost as an aside, reinforcing how the show prioritizes the Cao Cao storyline over the traditional Liu Bei focus of the novel.

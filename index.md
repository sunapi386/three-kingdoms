# New Three Kingdoms (新三国) Episode Guide

Episode summaries and historical significance notes for the 2010 Chinese TV series directed by Gao Xixi (高希希). 95 episodes covering the fall of the Han dynasty through the rise of the Sima clan.

Each episode includes a plot summary and a note separating real history from novelistic invention.
